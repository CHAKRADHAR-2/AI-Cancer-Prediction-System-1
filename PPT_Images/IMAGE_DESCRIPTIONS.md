## 🎨 **Design Guidelines**

### **Color Scheme**
- **Primary**: Medical Blue (#2563eb)
- **Secondary**: Emerald Green (#059669)
- **Accent**: Purple (#7c3aed)
- **Warning**: Orange (#ea580c)
- **Success**: Green (#16a34a)

### **Chart Types Recommended**
- **Performance Data**: Bar charts, line graphs
- **Accuracy Metrics**: Gauge charts, progress bars
- **Comparisons**: Side-by-side bar charts
- **Distributions**: Histograms, box plots
- **Geographic**: Maps with markers
- **System Architecture**: Flow diagrams, network graphs

### **Tools for Creation**
- **Charts**: Excel, Google Sheets, Chart.js, D3.js
- **Diagrams**: Draw.io, Lucidchart, Figma
- **Maps**: Google Maps, Mapbox, OpenStreetMap
- **Mockups**: Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch

### **Image Specifications**
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Format**: PNG for charts, SVG for diagrams
- **Background**: White or transparent
- **Font**: Professional sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica)
- **Size**: Optimized for PowerPoint slides
